# Literature Map

This repo keeps a clear claim boundary between established literature and EBC-specific diagnostics.

## Relational and internal time

- Don N. Page and William K. Wootters, "Evolution without evolution: Dynamics described by stationary observables," *Physical Review D* 27, 2885, 1983.
- Ekaterina Moreva et al., "Time from quantum entanglement: An experimental illustration," *Physical Review A* 89, 052122, 2014.
- Chiara Marletto and Vlatko Vedral, "Evolution without evolution, and without ambiguities," *Physical Review D* 95, 043510, 2017.

## Thermal / entropic time

- Alain Connes and Carlo Rovelli, "Von Neumann algebra automorphisms and time-thermodynamics relation in generally covariant quantum theories," 1994.

## Problem of time

- Bryce S. DeWitt, "Quantum Theory of Gravity. I. The Canonical Theory," *Physical Review* 160, 1113, 1967.
- Karel V. Kuchar, "Time and interpretations of quantum gravity," 1992.
- Edward Anderson, "Problem of Time in Quantum Gravity," 2012.

## Cold-atom analogue anchor

- Giovanni Barontini, "Emergence of Entropic Time in a Tabletop Wheeler-DeWitt Universe," 2025/2026 preprint and Physical Review Research line.

## BEC / Gross-Pitaevskii foundations

- F. Dalfovo, S. Giorgini, L. P. Pitaevskii, and S. Stringari, "Theory of Bose-Einstein condensation in trapped gases," *Reviews of Modern Physics* 71, 463, 1999.
- J. Rogel-Salazar, "The Gross-Pitaevskii Equation and Bose-Einstein condensates," 2013.

## Crystal growth / graphene simulation path

- Kinetic Monte Carlo models for epitaxial graphene island growth and growth kinetics.
- Phase-field crystal models for polycrystalline graphene, grain boundaries, and defect structures.

## Use in this repository

- Established physics sources motivate boundary, reduced-state, and clock-variable language.
- EBC equations define a simulation architecture.
- Fource and Darkness Functional are framework-specific diagnostics and must remain labeled as such.
